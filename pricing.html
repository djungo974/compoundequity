A Pro alert, as it arrives

When something major happens, you know at 7:30 AM or 5:30 PM, New York time.

We check twice a day, at 7:30 AM and 5:30 PM New York time, Monday to Saturday, and the alert arrives in the private Pro channel; what happens after Saturday 5:30 PM is covered on Monday at 7:30 AM, or in the Sunday issue. Questions

Before you subscribe.

Is this investment advice?

No. Metals & Machines is a research publication. We report and explain public information about three sectors. We don't know your situation, we don't manage money, and we never tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold. For advice on your own situation, talk to a licensed professional.

How do I receive it?

On Telegram, a free messaging app for phone and computer, or, for the free issue, by email. The free plan is a public channel you can join in one tap, or one email every Sunday. After you pay for Pro, our bot sends you a link to the private Pro channel; it lets in only the Telegram account that paid, so the link can't be passed on. Pro also arrives by email, at the address you pay with. Don't want the emails? Unsubscribe in one click: Telegram stays.

Telegram or email?

The free Sunday issue comes either way: one email, or one notification in a channel that keeps every past issue in one place. Pro comes both ways: the private Telegram channel, where an alert reaches your phone the moment it is sent, and an email to the address you pay with. Don't want the emails? Unsubscribe in one click: Telegram stays. Leaving the free issue takes one click too.

Is the content personalized?

No. Every subscriber on a plan gets exactly the same issue, at the same time. We don't offer calls, portfolio reviews or answers about your holdings, and our bot doesn't answer individual questions.

Does the author own what you write about?

The author holds positions in funds that invest in these sectors. Each issue ends with a disclosure of any positions related to its content, and the author follows a published personal trading policy: no buying or selling of anything mentioned in the 48 hours before and after publication. Full details are in our Disclosure policy.

How are signals chosen?

Software agents collect events from regulators, government feeds, company releases and the specialist press, and score each one from 1 to 5 for importance, with a written reason. Before anything is published, every figure must be found in its cited source and a second AI model checks each statement against the sources; every alert must come from an official source or be reported by two different outlets; these checks are automatic, anything that fails one is held back and not sent automatically, and any post can be removed. We publish our past signals and what happened next — including our mistakes — in the track record.

What if something in an issue is wrong?

We correct it in the next issue and log the correction in the track record. If you spot an error, please tell us.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Open our bot, tap My account, then Manage or cancel my subscription: it opens a secure page from our payment provider, Stripe. You can also cancel from your orders at link.com. Your access continues until the end of the period you've paid for, and new subscribers can get a full refund within 14 days of their first payment — see our Refund policy.
