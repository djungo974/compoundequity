Questions

Before you subscribe.

Is this investment advice?

No. Metals & Machines is a research publication. We report and explain public information about three sectors. We don't know your situation, we don't manage money, and we never tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold. For advice on your own situation, talk to a licensed professional.

How do I receive it?

On Telegram, a free messaging app for phone and computer. The free plan is a public channel you can join in one tap. After you pay for Pro, our bot sends you a link to the private Pro channel; it lets in only the Telegram account that paid, so the link can't be passed on.

Why Telegram and not email?

Because inboxes are already full of promotions and reminders. A channel delivers the issue as one notification, keeps every past issue in one place, and you can leave in one tap. You don't even give us an email address.

Is the content personalized?

No. Every subscriber on a plan gets exactly the same issue, at the same time. We don't offer calls, portfolio reviews or answers about your holdings, and our bot doesn't answer individual questions.

Does the author own what you write about?

The author holds positions in funds that invest in these sectors. Each issue ends with a disclosure of any positions related to its content, and the author follows a published personal trading policy: no buying or selling of anything mentioned in the 48 hours before and after publication. Full details are in our Disclosure policy.

How are signals chosen?

Software agents collect events from regulators, government feeds, company releases and the specialist press, and score each one from 1 to 5 for importance, with a written reason. Before anything is published, every figure must be found in its cited source and a second reader checks each statement against the sources; the editor can correct or stop any issue, and alerts go out only once the editor approves them. We publish our past signals and what happened next — including our mistakes — in the track record.

What if something in an issue is wrong?

We correct it in the next issue and log the correction in the track record. If you spot an error, please tell us.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Open our bot, tap My account, then Manage or cancel my subscription: it opens a secure page from our payment provider, Stripe. You can also cancel from your orders at link.com. Your access continues until the end of the period you've paid for, and new subscribers can get a full refund within 14 days of their first payment — see our Refund policy.
