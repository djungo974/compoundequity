Questions

Before you subscribe.

Is this investment advice?

No. [BRAND] is a research publication. We report and explain public information about three sectors. We don't know your situation, we don't manage money, and we never tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold. For advice on your own situation, talk to a licensed professional.

Is the content personalized?

No. Every subscriber on a plan gets exactly the same issue, at the same time. We don't offer calls, portfolio reviews or answers about your holdings.

Does the author own what you write about?

The author holds positions in funds that invest in these sectors. Each issue ends with a disclosure of any positions related to that day's content, and the author follows a published personal trading policy: no buying or selling of anything mentioned in the 48 hours before and after publication. Full details are in our Disclosure policy.

How are signals chosen?

Software agents collect events from official sources and score each one from 1 to 5 for importance, with a written reason. A human then checks every link and number against the source before anything is sent. We publish our past signals and what happened next — including our mistakes — in the track record.

What if something in a brief is wrong?

We correct it in the next issue and log the correction in the track record. If you spot an error, please tell us.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your account. Your access continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
