About

Metals & Machines is an independent research publication from Radar Signal Media LLC. Behind Metals & Machines

Built by an investor who builds AI systems.

Metals & Machines was started by an independent investor who builds AI automation systems for a living — workflows and multi-agent systems that read, sort and act on large amounts of information.

The author invests in these three sectors through European-listed ETFs, described in our Disclosure policy. Following them properly means reading regulators, government contract feeds, exchange filings and company releases across several countries, every week. Metals & Machines puts both skills together: agents do the reading, automatic checks hold back anything the sources don't support, and the editor can correct or stop any issue.

Metals & Machines is not a registered investment adviser and doesn't give personal advice. It tells every reader the same thing: what happened, why it could matter, and where to check it yourself.

The method

Agents read. Every fact is checked. The editor can stop anything.

Each step exists to catch a specific kind of mistake before it reaches you.

Collect

One collector per sector pulls new events from official sources and puts them in the same format: date, sector, company, event type, summary, source link. Duplicates are removed.

Score

Each event gets an importance score from 1 to 5, with a written reason, so the ranking can be checked rather than trusted.

Check

Automatic checks hold an issue back if a figure isn't in its source, a quote isn't in its source word for word, a second reader finds a statement the sources don't support, promotional or advice wording slips in, or the disclosure is missing.

Approve

The editor gets every draft on Saturday and can correct, approve or stop it. An issue nobody has approved goes out on Sunday only if every fact check passed. Alerts always need the editor's approval.
