About

One founder. A set of agents. A human check on everything.

[BRAND] is an independent research publication. There is no team of analysts behind it, and no advisers — just the approach described below.

The founder

I'm Férid.

I'm an independent investor, and I build AI automation systems for a living — workflows and multi-agent systems that read, sort and act on large amounts of information.

I invest in these three sectors myself, through funds that are listed in our Disclosure policy. Following them properly means reading regulators, government contract feeds, exchange filings and company releases across several countries, every day. [BRAND] puts my two skills together: agents do the reading, and I check what they find before it reaches you.

The method

Agents read. Rules check. A human signs off.

Each step exists to catch a specific kind of mistake before it reaches you.

Collect

One collector per sector pulls new events from official sources and puts them in the same format: date, sector, company, event type, summary, source link. Duplicates are removed.

Score

Each event gets an importance score from 1 to 5, with a written reason, so the ranking can be checked rather than trusted.

Check

An automatic check blocks the draft if a link is broken, a number doesn't match its source, promotional wording slips in, or the counter-thesis or disclosure is missing.

Approve

I review the draft every morning before it is sent. A draft that fails the automatic check is never sent.

Principles

Four commitments.

Same content for everyone

Every subscriber on a plan receives the same issue at the same time. Nothing is tailored to anyone.

A source for every number

Every figure links to where it comes from. If it can't be sourced, it isn't printed.

Mistakes are published

Past signals and what happened next — right or wrong — are logged in the public track record.

Positions are disclosed

Each issue lists the author's related positions, and the author follows a published personal trading policy.
