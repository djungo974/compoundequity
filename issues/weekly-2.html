HALEU deal signed as defense funding shrinks

Sent at 9:00 AM New York time to the free and Pro Telegram channels. The free part is below in full, word for word. The Pro part is shown in preview.

The week in 60 seconds

  • 🟢 Uranium — Urenco and Blykalla signed a HALEU enrichment agreement for Blykalla's first SEALER reactor, with deliveries expected in the early 2030s.
  • 🟠 Mining — Zimbabwe will tax lithium concentrate exports on the value of the lithium sulphate it could produce, not the concentrate's selling price.
  • 🟣 Defense & AI — Senate appropriators put nearly $9.4 billion for Golden Dome in a draft defense bill, well below the $17.5 billion request.

In Pro that week: This week's Pro deep dives cover the HALEU supply chain, Zimbabwe's lithium export tax and the fight over defense reconciliation money.

🟠 ⚛️ Uranium — Urenco and Blykalla sign HALEU enrichment deal

What happened: Urenco and Blykalla signed an agreement under which uranium feed supplied by Blykalla will be enriched at Urenco's HALEU facility at Capenhurst in the UK. The fuel will be used for Blykalla's first SEALER unit in the USA. …

🟠 ⛏️ Mining — Zimbabwe changes how it taxes lithium exports

What happened: Zimbabwe will tax lithium concentrate exports based on the value of the lithium sulphate that could be produced from them, not the concentrate's selling price. Finance Minister Mthuli Ncube said benchmark-grade 6% lithium concentrate will be converted at a ratio of five metric tons of concentrate to one ton of lithium sulphate for export tax purposes. …

🟠 🛡️ Defense & AI — Golden Dome funding cut in Senate draft bill

What happened: Senate appropriators pushed roughly half of the administration's $17 billion-plus Golden Dome request into a draft defense bill. The nearly $1.1 trillion proposal from the GOP-led Senate Appropriations Committee tagged nearly $9.4 billion for Golden Dome in its fiscal 2027 defense draft. …
