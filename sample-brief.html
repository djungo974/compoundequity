2 · Today's signals

Uranium 3/5
Cameco becomes exclusive buyer of Paducah's laser-enriched uranium

What happened: On September 18, 2026, Global Laser Enrichment (51% owned by Silex Systems, 49% by Cameco) signed an offtake agreement making Cameco the exclusive buyer and seller of all uranium products from its planned Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility in Kentucky, at pricing tied to Cameco's average realized long-term contract price.

Why it matters: It locks in a sales channel for a plant that doesn't exist yet, sparing Global Laser Enrichment the cost of its own sales team. But the facility still needs an enrichment license from the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, and production isn't expected before 2030 — this is a bet on the buildout, not a fix for today's supply.

Source: World Nuclear News, "Cameco in offtake agreement for US laser enrichment plant"
Mining 2/5
DOE puts $73 million behind four mining-technology test projects

What happened: On September 9, 2026, the Department of Energy's Office of Critical Minerals and Energy Innovation selected four projects — led by Innovative Wireless Technologies, the University of Arizona, the University of Missouri and Southern Methodist University — for a combined $73 million under its "Mine of the Future" initiative, to build domestic test grounds for new mining technology.

Why it matters: This funds technology testing and validation, not new mines. It's a small, early-stage bet on future domestic mining capacity — worth tracking for what these test grounds eventually validate, not for any near-term output.

Source: U.S. Department of Energy, official announcement
Defense 3/5
Pentagon's in-house AI platform crosses 2 million weekly logins

What happened: On September 23, 2026, the Defense Department's Chief Digital and AI Officer, Cameron Stanley, said GenAI.mil — which hosts Google's Gemini for Government, OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil and xAI's Grok for Government — had "over 2 million people in a week across all three models log in and use AI," up from about 300,000 users in its first week in December 2025.

Why it matters: This is an adoption number, not a capability one — it says how many Defense Department staff are logging into commercial chat tools for everyday work, not what those tools are being used for or how well. It's still a fast, real shift in how the department's civilian and military workforce operates day to day.

Source: Defense One, "GenAI.mil saw more than 2 million users in one week"

3 · Against the thesis

Uranium
A U.S. enrichment build-out still needs the regulator's sign-off

The Paducah facility's environmental impact statement was targeted for completion in September 2026, but the Nuclear Regulatory Commission's safety evaluation report isn't expected until January 2027, and the facility's license is still pending an uncontested mandatory hearing after that. The Cameco–Global Laser Enrichment deal above, like the U.S. domestic-enrichment story more broadly, depends on a multi-year regulatory and construction timeline — not a near-term supply fix.

Source: Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Global Laser Enrichment Facility Licensing
