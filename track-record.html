Track record

Every signal we publish, and what happened next.

A research publication should be judged on its record, not its promises. This log lists every signal we publish, its importance score, and how events actually unfolded — including the signals that turned out not to matter and the errors we corrected.

Nothing gets deleted

Entries are added, never removed. If a signal is revised, the original stays visible next to the update.

Corrections are dated

When we get a fact wrong, we say so in the next issue and log the correction here with the date.

This is not a performance record

We track whether signals were accurate and important — not whether anyone made or lost money on them.
