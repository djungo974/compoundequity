2. Disclosure in every issue

Every issue of the Daily Radar and the Weekly Wrap ends with a disclosure block that lists the author's positions connected to that day's content — or states "None" if there are none. The block names each fund by its full name and ISIN.

3. Personal trading

The author follows a published personal trading policy. In short: no buying or selling of any security or fund mentioned in an issue during the 48 hours before and the 48 hours after its publication.
